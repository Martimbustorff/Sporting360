## Sporting Clube de Portugal

Use exclusively the **new Sporting CP symbol supplied by the project owner on 06/09/2026** for S360 infographics.

Canonical supplied asset fingerprint:
- format: PNG
- dimensions: 1500×1500
- SHA-256: `b46aefecbaf6f1ff21ce7a5add3bbf75d31b031b73c4a96990e819c4ede10fae`
- visual: green `SCP` monogram above the green shield with white ornamental border and white lion

Rules:
1. Do not use the previous/classic Sporting crest in new S360 graphics.
2. Do not redraw or approximate the symbol with generative image tools when the canonical supplied asset is available.
3. Preserve proportions and transparent/background treatment; do not distort the shield.
4. Any replacement Sporting asset must be explicitly approved by the project owner and this fingerprint/document updated.
