# Notes for App Review (App Store Connect, "App Review Information")

S360 is an unofficial fan app for supporters of a Portuguese sports club. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the club (Sporting Clube de Portugal). The feature graphic and app name do not use the club crest. This is stated in the first paragraph of the App Store description and in the in-app Terms.

Team crests, fixtures, results, standings and statistics come from a third-party sports data provider (API-Football / API-Sports). News items show title, image and summary from public RSS feeds, with a link to the original article. Videos are embedded from the club's public video player.

Sign in: email and password, Google, or Sign in with Apple. Account deletion is inside the app: Home, Settings icon, "Apagar conta" (type APAGAR to confirm). A public deletion page is also available at https://s360-prognostico-download.web.app/apagar-conta

Analytics is off until the user opts in, and there is no tracking and no advertising. Crash reports are not linked to the user.

A test account will be entered by the developer in the "Sign-in required" fields of App Store Connect. The app is in Portuguese (pt-PT) only.
